## Brand & Style

The design system embodies a serene, restorative sanctuary tailored for privacy-first peer wellness and zero-knowledge communal support. The target audience comprises individuals seeking safe harbor for sensitive personal journeys, clinical peer dialogue, and identity-shielded belonging. The emotional landscape must evoke unconditional safety, lightness, cognitive calm, and absolute discretion without the sterile detachment of traditional enterprise cryptographic tools.

The aesthetic fuses **Modern Organic Minimalism** with **Calming Frosted Glassmorphism**:
- Crisp, clinical clarity through expansive, luminous white and atmospheric tint canvases.
- Ambient frosted glass surfaces (`backdrop-filter: blur(...)`) conveying gentle veiling, sanctuary, and cryptographic boundaries without visual weight or darkness.
- Deep empathetic reassurance delivered through sage, mint, tranquil cerulean, and nurturing rose accents.
- Utter absence of dark, intimidating technical metaphors; zero-knowledge proofs are framed as private sacred clearings rather than black-box encryption.

## Colors

The palette is strictly luminous, restorative, and air-filled. Dark mode and dense black backdrops are completely disallowed to maintain psychological clarity, clinical cleanliness, and warmth.

### Primary Sage & Botanical Mint
- **Primary Base (`#059669`)**: Core actions, validated zero-knowledge state anchors, and grounded navigation.
- **Primary Vibrant (`#10B981`)**: Interactive hover states and active confirmation indicators.
- **Primary Subtle / Glow (`#34D399`)**: Subtle status blurs, rings, and safe progress indications.
- **Primary Wash (`#ECFDF5`)**: Restorative container fills, ambient card fills, and active identity shields.

### Secondary Tranquil Azure
- **Secondary Base (`#0284C7`)**: Network verification badges, Midnight protocol links, and informational callouts.
- **Secondary Light (`#38BDF8`)**: Soft focus halos and interactive peer-presence pings.
- **Secondary Tint (`#E0F2FE`)**: Atmospheric canvas gradients and serene dialogue bubbles.

### Tertiary Supportive Blush
- **Tertiary Base (`#F43F5E`)**: Compassionate emergency triggers, alert states, and heart/empathy reactions.
- **Tertiary Soft (`#FB7185`)**: Micro-interaction accents, active affinity tokens, and emotional tag borders.
- **Tertiary Wash (`#FFF1F2`)**: Subtle warning backdrops, empathetic support cards, and peer gratitude banners.

### Canvas & Neutral Architecture
- **Canvas Root (`#FFFFFF`)**: Base luminous plane.
- **Atmospheric Layer (`#F8FAFC`)**: Structural ground for cards and section segmentation.
- **Organic Tint 1 (`#F0FDF4`)**: Soft sage wash for private circle workspaces.
- **Organic Tint 2 (`#F0F9FF`)**: Soft sky wash for verification flows.
- **Text Primary (`#0F172A`)**: Slate deep-neutral ensuring high-contrast AA/AAA legibility.
- **Text Secondary (`#475569`)**: Balanced slate for metadata and auxiliary labels.
- **Border / Outline Soft (`#E2E8F0`)**: Hairline boundary defining structural panels alongside translucent specular highlights.

## Typography

Typography prioritizes approachable geometric precision, warm rounded terminals, and clear rhythmic legibility using **Plus Jakarta Sans**. 

- **Headlines & Display:** Designed with subtle negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) to ground introspective titles with solid presence.
- **Body Rhythm:** Ample line height (`1.6` to `1.75`) reduces eye fatigue during emotional reading sessions.
- **Privacy Badges & Micro-Copy:** Specially engineered `label-zk-proof` ensures zero-knowledge proof tokens, hash masks, and cryptographic validation badges remain legible and crisp when rendered within miniature pill wrappers.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout uses an airy 12-column fluid grid system on desktop that shifts gracefully to an 8-column layout on tablet, and a focused 4-column layout on mobile.

- **Breathing Clearances:** Margin and padding values skew expansive to eliminate density anxiety. Screen edges never crowd content; cards float within contextual background washes (`#F8FAFC` blending into `#F0FDF4` or `#F0F9FF`).
- **Responsive Adaptations:**
  - **Mobile (<768px):** Outer margin collapses to `1rem`, cards adopt single-column full-width flows with `1rem` inner padding. Floating action drawers replace persistent sidebars.
  - **Tablet (768px–1024px):** Split-view layouts allow room for private session sidebars with an anchored `1.5rem` gutter.
  - **Desktop (>1024px):** Maximum reading boundaries cap at `1280px` centered, ensuring peer circle discussions and identity verification widgets maintain an intimate, focus-centric field of view.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses frosted glassmorphism layered over ethereal ambient shadows tinted with botanical sage and sky tones. Opaque, heavy black shadows are banned.

- **Layer 0 (Canvas Base):** Luminous surface in `#FFFFFF` or atmospheric ambient gradient (`#F8FAFC` to `#F0FDF4`).
- **Layer 1 (Frosted Glass Container):** Background fill of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.75)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a subtle light reflection border of `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`. Ambient shadow: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.06), 0 2px 8px -2px rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.04)`.
- **Layer 2 (Elevated Active Card / Circle Modal):** Background fill of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`. Elevated shadow: `0 20px 40px -12px rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.08), 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Layer 3 (Floating Privacy Controls & Tooltips):** Pure crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) with halo outline `1px solid rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.2)` and omnidirectional shadow: `0 12px 36px 0 rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary communicates gentleness, psychological safety, and continuous organic flow:
- Standard elements (interactive fields, structural panels, and cards) leverage **Level 2 Roundedness** (`0.5rem` base, scaling to `1rem` on container components and `1.5rem` on focal modal panels).
- All zero-knowledge verification badges, user anonymity toggles, status pills, and action chips strictly use full rounded radii (`border-radius: 9999px`) to reinforce the metaphor of a secure, unbroken protective circle.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Sanctuary Button:** Solid sage `#059669` fill with crisp white text. Hover transitions to `#10B981` with an ambient glow shadow `0 6px 20px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.25)`. Border-radius is `0.75rem`. Focus ring uses an azure offset `ring-2 ring-[#38BDF8]`.
- **Secondary Protocol Button:** Soft tinted fill in `#F0FDF4` or `#E0F2FE` with contrasting text (`#059669` or `#0284C7`), paired with a hairline perimeter in `rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.15)`.
- **Tertiary Care Action:** Ghost styling with blush-tinted hover states (`#FFF1F2`), used for vulnerable peer signals, reports, or emergency relief access.

### Zero-Knowledge Status Badges & Chips
- Fully rounded pill containers (`padding: 0.25rem 0.75rem`) featuring a dual indicator:
  - **Verified ZK Anonymous Badge:** Pale mint ground (`#ECFDF5`), deep sage text (`#059669`), leading with a glowing micro-dot (`#10B981`) pulsing gently to denote live cryptographic shielding via the Midnight blockchain.
  - **Pseudonymous Circle Chip:** Subtle sky blue ground (`#E0F2FE`), text in `#0284C7`, bordered by hairline translucent cyan.

### Cards & Frosted Clearings
- Standard member cards employ frosted acrylic glass styling (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`) with `1.25rem` padding, subtle borders (`rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`), and soft tinted shadows. Hover elevates card scale imperceptibly (`scale-[1.01]`) while intensifying the soft mint ambient back-glow.

### Form Inputs & Textareas
- Base surfaces are translucent milk-glass (`rgba(248, 250, 252, 0.7)`), inset with smooth `1px` borders (`#E2E8F0`).
- Focus state instantly expands into crisp white with a sage glow border (`#10B981`) and faint azure halo (`rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.2)`).
- Helper microcopy appears in soft slate `#475569` with optional lock micro-icons confirming field-level cryptographic encryption.

### Checkboxes & Toggle Switches
- **Toggles (Shield Actuators):** Fully rounded pill track. In active zero-knowledge mode, the track transitions from slate tint (`#E2E8F0`) to vibrant emerald (`#10B981`), sliding a white circular thumb with smooth spring inertia.
- **Checkboxes:** Rounded squares (`rounded-md`) bordered in `#CBD5E1`. When selected, fills with `#059669` and displays a crisp white organic checkmark.

### Additional Domain Components
- **Veil Shield Indicator:** A persistent top-bar pill showing current Midnight zk-SNARK connection status, masked wallet anonymity level, and cryptographic session life.
- **Supportive Reaction Bubbles:** Floating pill-shaped empathy counters (Heart, Warmth, Presence) wrapped in `#FFF1F2` with rose borders (`#FB7185`), allowing one-tap silent solidarity without revealing participant identity.
